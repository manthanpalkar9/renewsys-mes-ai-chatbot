3. Executive Summary
Renewsys India Pvt. Ltd.?wants a conversational assistant (the ?Renewsys Chatbot?) that lets 
MES users at the Khopoli plant ask questions in plain English and receive information 
currently obtained by manually querying or reporting from the Jinchen MES database. Phase 1 
targets a single plant, approximately 15?20 total users, and 3?4 concurrent users.
The stakeholder intent, in the respondent?s own words (A4): ?Instead of retrieving data directly 
from the database we can take help from this chat what it can translate from plain English to 
the queries and generate the required fields.? This is a stated business need, not a technical 
design; how it is technically achieved (an LLM, a semantic layer, a Text-to-SQL pipeline) is a 
matter for the implementation team to propose, and any such proposal is explicitly labeled 
PROPOSED TECHNICAL SOLUTION throughout this document.
The respondents are MES users, not the developers of the underlying MES ? which was built 
by a third-party (Jinchen) team whose server the respondents describe as being ?in China? and 
something ?we do not handle.? As a result, the database engine, schema, stored procedures, 
connection architecture, several business-data definitions, KPI formulas, and portions of the 
security/network environment are not currently known and are documented here as TBD 
rather than assumed.
This third revision of the SRS makes several corrections identified during review:
1. Email and Microsoft Teams remain identified as in-scope systems (Q1), not 
reclassified as Future Phase ? their integration detail is TBD, which is a different status 
from being out of scope.
2. The complete 139-field questionnaire traceability matrix replaces any earlier 
?representative sample? table (Section 38).
3. The Source-of-Truth register no longer uses the word ?presumed? ? Jinchen MES is 
documented as a candidate, not yet validated source for each domain.

11. Query Interpretation and Clarification
Confirmed (U1): Ask a clarification question rather than guess. The system shall:
1. Detect ambiguity in the incoming question.
2. Ask a clarifying question rather than proceed on a guess.
3. Avoid generating a potentially misleading query while the interpretation is unresolved.
4. Execute the query only once the interpretation is sufficiently clear.
Examples of ambiguity requiring clarification (Proposed, illustrative): ambiguous date range, 
ambiguous line, ambiguous shift, ambiguous product/material, ambiguous quantity definition, 
ambiguous KPI reference.
Must-not-answer criteria (E7) and clarification-trigger criteria beyond U1 (E8) were 
both answered ?Over to you? ? classified AMBIGUOUS, i.e., delegated to the implementation 
team. Any resulting policy is a PROPOSED safe-answering approach, not a stakeholder-
confirmed rule: avoid answering when the requested information is outside authorized data, 
the required field is unavailable, the query remains ambiguous after clarification, confidence is 
insufficient, the data source cannot support the answer, or the requested operation is not 
permitted (e.g., a write).
The system must never invent an answer when underlying business definitions are unknown 
(e.g., Material, process loss, rework ? see Section 20).

12.2 Important Caveat on ?Fully Accessible?
?Fully accessible? (F4) does not remove the need for least-privilege validation, credential 
security, a database permissions review, and production security approval before go-live. It 
reflects the absence of a named restriction from this respondent, not an instruction to bypass 
standard access governance. No table-level restriction is invented; any governance layer 
applied on top of F4 is a PROPOSED TECHNICAL SOLUTION.

39. Acceptance Criteria
Phase 1 may be considered ready for stakeholder acceptance review when, at minimum:
? Every CONFIRMED requirement in this document (Sections 9?20) is implemented and 
demonstrable against either the mock data contract (Track A, Section 24) or validated 
real MES data (Track B).
? Every capability whose underlying business definition is currently TBD (process loss, 
rework rate, WIP, first pass yield, quantity semantics ? Section 14/20) is either 
validated and implemented, or explicitly deferred with stakeholder sign-off.
? SQL execution is verified, by testing, to be restricted to SELECT statements only via the 
dedicated read-only account, independent of any additional proposed safety layer.
? RBAC correctly restricts SQL/source visibility to Admin/IT users (UAT-08).
? Session-scoped conversational memory correctly resolves at least the follow-up pattern 
demonstrated in UAT-09.
? The profit question (UAT-05) is not answered with a fabricated value; the system 
instead communicates that the required financial data source is unconfirmed.
=== PAGE 71 ===
? The Open Issues register (Section 35) has been reviewed with IT/DBA/MES 
vendor/business stakeholders, and each item is either resolved or explicitly accepted as 
an open risk by the stakeholder-named approver (?Developer,? V3).
? Email and Microsoft Teams integration scope has been revisited with the stakeholder to 
either (a) obtain sufficient detail to implement a defined Phase 1 boundary, or (b) 
obtain explicit confirmation that they are deferred beyond Phase 1 ? neither of which 
this SRS currently assumes on its own.
No acceptance criterion in this section asserts a specific numeric performance target, KPI 
formula, or timeline commitment beyond what the questionnaire itself supports.
